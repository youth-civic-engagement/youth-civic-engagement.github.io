Civil rights legislation dashboard

State legislation reviewed for individual impact

A state-by-state review of measures that expressly classify people by Chinese nationality or citizenship, or use broader legal categories that include them. Tier 1 can reach lawful permanent residents (LPRs) and/or U.S. citizens. Tier 2 reaches visa applicants, visa holders, nonresident aliens, or other non-LPR foreign nationals without reaching citizens or LPRs.
