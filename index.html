About

Youth Civic Engagement is a youth-led initiative focused on increasing civic participation, voter education, and community organizing. We believe young people have the power to drive meaningful change when given tools, mentorship, and a platform.

Our mission is to make civic learning accessible, inclusive, and action-oriented.

Projects

  • Civic Workshops – Interactive sessions on local government, advocacy, and media literacy.
  • Community Mapping – Youth researchers documenting local issues and assets.
  • Get Out The Vote – Peer-to-peer voter registration and education campaigns.
