Federal legislation under review

A separate review of pending federal measures that may directly restrict people based on Chinese nationality or may reach them through a broader affiliation-based classification. Tier 1 can reach lawful permanent residents (LPRs) and/or U.S. citizens. Tier 2 reaches visa applicants, visa holders, nonresident aliens, or other non-LPR foreign nationals without reaching citizens or LPRs.
